# Product Marketing Context — ClearWork

## Product Overview

**One-line:** ClearWork is the all-in-one business workspace for Indian freelancers and agencies — from lead capture to project done.

**What it does:** ClearWork replaces 5+ disconnected tools with one clean, end-to-end workflow. Freelancers and agencies use it to manage leads, send tracked proposals, get contracts e-signed via OTP, issue GST invoices with UPI payments embedded, and track projects to completion — all in one place, all connected.

**Product category:** Freelancer / agency business software (not a CRM alone — a full business operations platform)

**Product type:** SaaS, B2B2C

**Business model:** Freemium — Free plan forever (up to 5 clients, 10 projects, 3 proposals/month). Pro ₹149/mo (up to 30 clients, unlimited proposals, white-label portal, AI drafter, priority support). Studio ₹649/mo (unlimited everything, white-label on your domain, multi-currency, team payouts). Every new account gets a free 15-day Pro trial — no credit card needed.

**Full workflow covered:**
1. Lead CRM — capture leads from Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, referrals, enquiry forms, intake forms, and lead campaigns; pipeline view, win rates, follow-up dates; ENQUIRY → PROPOSAL_SENT → NEGOTIATING → CLIENT stage transitions
2. Proposals — branded proposals with scope, pricing, timeline; shareable tracked link; push notification on open; AI drafting
3. E-sign Contracts — one-click convert proposal → contract; OTP e-signature; legally binding under IT Act 2000; no DocuSign needed
4. GST Invoices — auto-filled from contract; CGST/SGST/IGST auto-detected by client state; TDS 194J/194C flag; payment link embedded
5. UPI Payments — client pays via UPI, card, net banking, or EMI in one tap directly from the invoice
6. Client Portal — branded portal per client with all proposals, contracts, invoices in one link; no login needed for client
7. Project Tracking — group all docs, time entries, and expenses per project; see profit per engagement; task board with client-visible toggle; team task allocation with payout recording
8. Automations — WhatsApp + email reminders for overdue invoices; auto-onboarding after contract sign; visual builder
9. Project Delivery — project updates feed visible in client portal; change request workflow with auto-invoice for approved changes; deliverable file sharing with version history; client task visibility per toggle
10. Project Sign-off & Reviews — OTP-verified project sign-off from client portal; post-project star rating + written review; reviews published to freelancer's public profile

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Indian service businesses — freelancers (designers, developers, consultants, photographers, content writers, video editors, marketing professionals), small agencies (2–20 person teams), and solo consultants

**Secondary:** Any India-based service business that sends proposals, signs contracts, and invoices clients — including tutors, coaches, architects, chartered accountants, and boutique studios

**Geography:** India (GST-registered or planning to be; UPI as primary payment rail)

**Decision maker:** The freelancer themselves, agency founder, or solo consultant owner

**Stage:** Anyone from "just starting out" (free plan fits) to "running a growing agency" (Pro/Studio). The free plan is designed to be genuinely useful forever — not crippled to force an upgrade.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core challenge:** Indian service businesses (freelancers, consultants, agencies) are running their business across 5+ separate tools — Notion/Trello for leads, Google Docs/Canva for proposals, WhatsApp/email for contracts, Zoho Books/Tally for invoices, Razorpay link for payments, and spreadsheets to track everything. Every tool is disconnected. Data gets re-entered. Clients get a scattered, unprofessional experience.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- Global tools (HoneyBook, Bonsai, Dubsado) are built for Western markets — no GST support, no UPI, no OTP e-sign under IT Act 2000
- Indian tools (Refrens, Zoho Books) handle invoicing but not proposals, contracts, or the full lead-to-payment workflow
- DocuSign is expensive and overkill for a single freelancer
- WhatsApp chasing overdue payments manually is embarrassing and time-consuming

**What it costs them:**
- Hours per week re-entering data across tools
- Lost deals from unprofessional proposals (Google Doc shared via WhatsApp)
- Delayed payments from manual follow-ups
- GST filing errors from scattered records
- Looking "small" and unorganized to clients

**Emotional tension:** "I'm good at my craft but terrible at running my business. My clients deserve better, and so do I."

## Positioning & Differentiation

**Primary positioning:** The all-in-one business platform for Indian service businesses. Not a CRM. Not just invoicing. The complete workflow from lead to project done — built natively for India.

**Key differentiators:**
1. **End-to-end connected** — Lead → Proposal → Contract → Invoice → Payment → Project; data flows automatically, nothing re-entered
2. **India-first** — GST auto-calculation (CGST/SGST/IGST by state), UPI + card payments, OTP e-sign under IT Act 2000, WhatsApp reminders
3. **Client experience** — Branded client portal; professional proposals; signed contracts; paid invoices — clients feel they're working with a real business
4. **Free forever, no catch** — The free plan is permanently free with real functionality; Pro trial gives 15 days on the paid tier to evaluate before committing

**Alternatives users come from:** Zoho Books, Refrens, Google Docs + Razorpay, HoneyBook, Bonsai, Dubsado
