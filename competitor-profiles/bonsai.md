## India Gap

- USD-only pricing — Indian freelancers pay ~2.5–4x fair market rate after conversion
- No GST fields in invoices
- No UPI payment links
- No IT Act e-sign (uses DocuSign-style signing which isn't IT Act compliant)

- Zoom acquisition creates uncertainty — product direction unclear, layoffs possible
