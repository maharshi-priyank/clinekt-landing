## Competitive Implications for ClearWork

**Zoho's role**: Zoho Invoice is a billing tool, not a client workflow tool. It does the money part but nothing before it (proposals, contracts, client acquisition).

**Where ClearWork wins**:
- Full workflow from lead to payment — Zoho Invoice starts at invoice
- Contracts and IT Act e-sign — Zoho has none
- Proposal builder with client approval — Zoho has none
- No scary data-deletion policy for inactive accounts
- No confusing ecosystem upsell pressure

**The Zoho trap**: Many Indian freelancers start with Zoho Invoice because it's free, then hit the limits and realize it doesn't handle proposals/contracts. ClearWork is the natural next step AND can replace Zoho Invoice entirely.
