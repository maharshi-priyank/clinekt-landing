A — Hub & Spoke Scatter

WhatsApp, Canva, Sheets, UPI scatter around a glowing center question. As you scroll, they grey out and fly offscreen. Rupway's dashboard fades in — same transition as Torckit. Most cinematic, highest impact on first impression.

B — Chaos → Clean Pipeline

A chaotic multi-tool flow animates on screen as you enter. As you scroll, the messy tools collapse and morph into Rupway's clean 5-step pipeline. More story-driven, excellent at showing the transformation. Works great for freelancers who instantly recognise the chaos.

C — Dark Dramatic Spotlight

Opens dark: scattered faded tools barely visible. As you scroll, a spotlight reveals Rupway glowing in the center. Product UI slides up dramatically. Premium, bold, unlike any Indian SaaS landing page right now. High risk, high reward.

My recommendation: Option A — closest to what you've seen from Torckit, most proven for SaaS, and maps perfectly to Rupway's pain story (4 broken tools → one solution). We keep Rupway's indigo brand identity. Option B is close second if you want something more original.
